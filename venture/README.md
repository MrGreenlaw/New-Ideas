## Verdict in one paragraph
Of 35+ candidates across 7 territories, the strongest evidence-backed opportunity is **Keelson**:
a broker-neutral tariff exposure and refund-rights system of record for ~35K mid-market US
importers, led by industrial manufacturers/distributors facing the Section 232 "15% metal cliff".
It sells volatility-proof value (evidence + deadlines), not bets on tariff levels. Base case is a
$0.5–2B company; a $10B+ path exists but is low-probability (~5%, D). **Nothing is customer-validated
yet** — the 30-day plan exists to prove or kill it for under $100K.
