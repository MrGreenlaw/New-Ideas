# 07 — Hiring Specifications

Optimise for builders, sellers and customer understanding. No executive titles before $3M ARR.

## 1. Licensed Customs Broker — Founding Team (Head of Trade Operations)
- **When:** contract by week 2; full-time with 1–3% equity by day 100.
- **Why:** determinations constitute customs business under 19 CFR 111 and must be made or supervised by a licensee; investors and customers require a named licensee (F, D analysts).
- **Responsibilities:** review and sign every finding; design the review workflow and evidence standard; serve as licensed officer for the corporate licence application; train the classification checker via adjudications; front customer technical calls.
- **Must have:** active individual customs broker licence; 5+ years in entry and classification for industrial goods (ch. 72–76, 84–85); hands-on Section 232 derivative and Section 301 work since 2025; protests and PSCs filed.
- **Nice:** drawback; CIT litigation support; supervised a small team.
- **Red flags:** only e-commerce/parcel experience; uncomfortable with software-first workflows.
- **Where to find:** NCBFAA and regional broker associations; LinkedIn "Licensed Customs Broker" + industrial importers; brokers at firms acquired in the 2025–26 consolidation.

## 2. Founding Engineer #2 (Full-stack / Data)
- **When:** month 2–3.
- **Responsibilities:** ingestion pipelines, ledger, reviewer UI, supplier portal, security posture.
- **Must have:** shipped B2B data products end-to-end; Postgres; Python or TypeScript; care about correctness and audit logs.
- **Nice:** tax/compliance software (Avalara-type), document AI, SOC 2.

## 3. Trade Analyst / Customer Success (first non-founder operator)
- **When:** at ~15 customers.
- **Responsibilities:** onboarding, ACE access coaching, supplier declaration collection, scan QA, customer check-ins, case studies.
- **Must have:** 2+ years trade compliance at an importer or broker; excellent written communication.
- **Path:** sponsor for the broker exam (22% pass rate means we should grow our own licensees).

## 4. First Account Executive
- **When:** ~30 customers and a documented founder playbook with ≥25% scan→Ledger conversion.
- **Must have:** sold SaaS to CFOs at $30–500M industrial/distribution companies; comfortable with 8–14-week cycles and legal review.
- **Comp:** ~50/50 base/variable; quota tied to Ledger ARR, not success fees.

## 5. Trade Counsel (retainer, not hire)
- **When:** week 1.
- **Scope:** 19 CFR 111 opinion; contract templates (POA scope, direction-to-pay, clawback, caps, tail); prior-disclosure workflow; CIT filing partnership for customers.
