# 03 — Customer Discovery Interview Guide (45 minutes)

Rules: ask about the past, not hypotheticals ("tell me about the last time…"); no pitching until
minute 35; record verbatim quotes; never lead. Log every answer in the CRM. Target 25 interviews in
weeks 1–3.

## Part 1 — Context (5 min)
1. Walk me through how a shipment gets from your supplier to your warehouse — who touches the customs part?
2. Roughly how much did you pay in duty in the last 12 months? How has that changed since 2024?
3. Who owns duty as a number — ops, finance, or nobody?

## Part 2 — Last painful event (15 min)
4. Tell me about the last time a tariff change hit you. What happened, step by step?
5. How did you work out which products were affected? How long did it take? Who did it?
6. Tell me about the last question from CBP (CF-28/29), your auditor or your CFO about duty.
7. What happened with your IEEPA refunds? Who filed? Did any entries fall outside the window?
8. For Section 232: how do you know the metal content of your derivative products today? Where is that evidence kept?

## Part 3 — Current solution and spend (10 min)
9. Who is your broker? How are they paid? When did you last change broker, and why?
10. What do you spend per year on brokerage, bonds, consultants, lawyers and internal staff for customs?
11. What have you tried that didn't work (software, consultants, internal projects)?
12. If you could wave a wand over one part of this, what would it be?

## Part 4 — Economics and buying (5 min)
13. If someone found $X of recoverable duty, who would have to approve working with them? What would they need to see?
14. What would make you nervous about giving someone read-only ACE report access?
15. Would your suppliers give you a signed metal-content or origin declaration if you asked? Have you asked?

## Part 5 — Test (5 min; only now describe the offer)
16. Show the sample Exposure Report. "What here is useful? What is noise? What's missing?"
17. "Would you grant ACE Reports access for a free scan this month?" (Record yes/no — this is the E2 metric.)
18. "If the scan found more than $50K, would you pay $2,500 for it / $X per month for the ledger?" (Van Westendorp: too cheap / cheap / expensive / too expensive.)

## After the call (log within 1 hour)
- Pain score 1–5 (evidence-based, not stated)
- Duty spend; broker; compliance headcount
- ACE access: yes / maybe / no
- Supplier-data willingness: yes / maybe / no
- Price anchors given
- Best verbatim quote
- Referral names offered
