# 08 — Partnership Targets

Categories and selection criteria. Named firms are examples of the category drawn from the research
record, not confirmed partners.

| Category | Why | Examples / where to find | Deal shape | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Independent customs brokers** | Channel + licensed supervision; neutralises "replace my broker" | Small/regional NCBFAA members; brokers who lost share to Flexport | White-label scan + 20% rev-share 24 months; they keep entries | P0 (E5) |
| **Trade-law firms** | Protests, CIT suits, prior disclosure; referral both ways | Firms active in IEEPA/301 FL litigation (visible on CIT dockets and client alerts) | Referral agreement; filing-ready package handoff; no fee-splitting that violates bar rules (counsel to structure) | P0 |
| **CPA firms / fractional CFOs with manufacturing practices** | Trusted access to CFOs; accrual pain | Regional top-100 CPA firms' manufacturing & distribution groups; fractional-CFO networks | Referral fee; free scans for their clients | P0 |
| **Industry associations** | Warm access, content distribution | NAW, Industrial Fasteners Institute, PTDA, HARDI, NAED, AHFA, MEMA; MEP centres | Sponsored webinars on 232/301; member discount | P1 |
| **ABI software vendor** | Filing capability without building ABI | CBP ABI vendor list (Sept 2025): Descartes, CargoWise, Magaya/AceLynk, Customs City | Usage-based filing API; data-ownership clause; two vendors evaluated | P1 (month 4–9) |
| **ERP partners/consultancies** | Item master + BOM data; distribution | NetSuite, Epicor, SYSPRO, Acumatica partner ecosystems | Integration + marketplace listing; consultant referral fees | P1 |
| **Bill-of-lading data providers** | Prospecting | ImportGenius, Panjiva, ImportYeti | Subscription | P0 |
| **Sureties / bond agents** | Bond-sufficiency trigger leads; later bond placement | Customs surety agencies | Lead sharing; commission later | P2 |
| **AI-for-brokers vendors** | Avoid war; possible data partnerships | Amari, Gaia, Altana | Integration where their tools sit at our partner brokers | P2 |
