**Keelson** — the tariff exposure and refund-rights system of record for mid-market importers.

**Problem.** US duties ~$195B in FY2025 (A/B). The legal basis has changed 50+ times since January
2025 (B); IEEPA was struck down, Section 122 was struck and expired, and Section 301 forced-labour
tariffs are before a sceptical trade court (A/B). Every change creates per-SKU determinations and
perishable refund rights — and in July 2026 only importers who had sued received refunds on finally
liquidated IEEPA entries (A/B). ~35K mid-market importers (C) manage this through brokers paid per
entry and spreadsheets.

**Product.** 14-day scan of ACE entry data → recoverable duty with deadlines, Section 232 cliff
exposure (0% vs 25% of full value at the 15% metal line, A), and rights to preserve. Converts to a
Ledger subscription: SKU evidence, supplier certifications, rights calendar, accrual reporting.
Licensed brokers sign every determination; the customer's broker keeps filing.

**Why we win.** Broker-neutral (Flexport audits itself; we don't). Industrial BOM depth where
recovery start-ups focus on e-commerce. Compounding assets: supplier evidence reusable across
importers, outcome data, deadline-bearing workflow.
