## Key facts
- LBNL Queued Up 2026 (data through end-2025): ~2,061 GW active (1,312 GW gen, 749 GW storage), ~10% decline YoY; >750 GW withdrew in 2025 (A-/B).
- FERC Order 2023 cluster studies; PJM ~480-day study commitment (B).
- FERC June 18, 2026: Section 206 show-cause orders to all six RTOs/ISOs on large-load (≥50 MW, >69 kV) integration rules (A, McGuireWoods, White & Case).
- Utility large-load pipelines: AEP 56 GW signed by 2030; Dominion ~53.8 GW contracted; Oncor 186 GW of requests (B). Paces argues much is duplicative/speculative.
- PJM 2027/28 BRA cleared at cap $333.44/MW-day, 6,623 MW short of reliability requirement; ~$16.4B cost, ~40% attributed to data centers (A, PJM).
- Transformers (WoodMac via pv magazine, May 2026): GSU lead times >160 weeks; 30% power-transformer shortfall; prices +77–95% since 2019; shortfall narrowing to ~5% by 2030 (B).
- Duke "Rethinking Load Growth": 76–126 GW headroom at 0.25–1% curtailment (A).
- Emerald AI: $150M Series A at $1.05B valuation (Aug 25, 2026) (A). GridCARE $64M Series A May 2026 (A). Google demand-response deals with I&M and TVA (A). Tapestry (X) multiyear deal with PJM (A). Enverus acquired Pearl Street (A). Paces $11M A. Utilidata $100M C. Camus $25M+.

- ~100 GW onsite gas planned for data centers vs ~2 GW operating (B/C).
