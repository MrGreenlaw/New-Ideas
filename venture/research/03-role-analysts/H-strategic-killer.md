# H — Strategic Killer: Customs (T1) vs Property Tax (T2) (Sonnet analyst, 2026-09-30)

Caveat: search quota exhausted after 3 queries; T1 claims sourced, T2 claims from prior knowledge [unverified].

Grades: T1 = C+ (real pain, perishable wedge, crowded seed field). T2 = C (proven but slow, seasonal, no moat).

## T1 — key attacks
- **Cause of death**: wedge (contingency audit riding IEEPA refunds) expires ~Q1–Q2 2027 before system of record exists; converting audit clients to brokerage is hard (POA lock-in); lumpy back-loaded cash → runway dies month 18–24.
- **Most dangerous competitor: Flexport** — ~$2.5B raised; AI customs auditor + automated tariff refunds launched Feb 26, 2026; claims 0.2% filing error rate; bundles into freight. Others: Zonos ($69M; CBP CSOP pilot; acquired Evolve Trade Services brokerage Apr 2026), Tarifflo (YC S2026), Caspian ($5.4M), Forge (AI-native forwarder/broker).
- **Tech risk**: LLM classification commoditises within ~12 months; CBP automation (CAPE, CSOP) lowers licensed-broker moat.
- **Reg risk**: tariff churn makes retrospective eligibility uncertain; rollback shrinks base.
- **Distribution risk**: "your broker overcharged you" attacks the person they call every day; fear of CBP scrutiny.
- **60-day kill evidence**: <3 of 20 importers grant data access; brokers already filed CAPE; competitor contingency <10–12%; sample audits find <0.3–0.5% of duty recoverable outside IEEPA; no one pays for subscription; broker partner impossible within 6 months.

## T2 — key attacks
- Services margin trap; seasonal; cash 6–24 months after work; small tickets ($3–40K per win at $2–25M assets); hearing labour doesn't scale; Ownwell can add commercial at marginal cost; assessor-side AI erodes lag arbitrage; attorney/agent requirements vary by state.

## Verdict
**Pursue T1, modified**: (1) don't become the broker first — partner with a licensed broker, own software + audit layer; (2) narrow the wedge to what commodity AI/Flexport can't easily copy: drawback, USMCA preference claims, Section 232 metal-content valuation, first-sale — all require SKU/BOM data from ERP; skip IEEPA refund chasing; (3) sell through existing brokers/CPAs/trade counsel as white-label; (4) contingency to land + annual subscription at signing. T2 not worth absorbing; borrow its contingency-pipeline ops lesson.
