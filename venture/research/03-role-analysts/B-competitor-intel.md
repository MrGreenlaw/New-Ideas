## Answers
1. No well-funded AI-native licensed broker owns the mid-market yet; Caspian is the closest (licensed, but $5.4M); Flexport is the well-funded broker but forwarder-first.
2. **White space**: SKU-level classification/origin system of record + continuous monitoring + filing for $250K–$25M duty importers; **broker independence** (Flexport's "audit your broker" is conflicted); non-IEEPA recurring value.
3. Complaints (thin evidence, C): poor communication, delays, hidden fees, opacity.
4. Most dangerous: Flexport > Caspian > Altana/Cervo (commoditises AI for every incumbent).
5. At $10M ARR: Flexport bundles free audits; Caspian raises A; Altana/Amari/Gaia arm rivals; incumbents match pricing; Descartes/WiseTech add agents or acquire.
