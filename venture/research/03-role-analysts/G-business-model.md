## Recommended pricing
- **Recovery (a)**: tiered on cash received: 25% ≤$100K; 20% $100–500K; 15% $500K–2M; 10% >$2M. Drop to 10–15% if client signs brokerage POA. Only statutorily open windows (PSC pre-liquidation; protest 180 days post-liquidation; drawback 5 years).
- **Brokerage (b)**: $95/formal entry (first 10 lines), $5/extra line, ISF $45, PGA $25; $500/mo minimum; floor ~$75/entry.
- **Subscription (c)**: Core $1,200/mo (<$1M duty); Growth $2,500/mo ($1–5M); Scale $4,500–8,000/mo (>$5M). Waive 50–100% when bundled with brokerage.
- **Later (d)**: bond commissions (small), ongoing drawback, defer FTZ/financing.

## COGS / gross margin
Per entry year 1 ~$31 (15 min broker review, LLM+ABI $4, E&O+support $12) → ~67% GM at $95; year 3 ~$15 (4 min exception review) → ~84%. Stress: flagged entries 25%+ → ~55%. Contingency 55–65% → 70–80%. Subscription 65–75% → 80–85%. Model 25–40% of claims recovering nothing.

## CAC
Founder outbound $6–15K; partnerships $3–8K + 10–20% rev-share; content $2–6K at maturity. Blended ~$8K yr1 → ~$5K at scale. Brokerage cycle 2–6 months; audit 3–9 weeks.

## Contracts
Limited annual POA terminable on 30 days; contingency on cash received only; per-claim caps; 12–24 month pro-rata clawback as credit; 12–18 month tail; client warrants document accuracy; liability cap at 12 months' fees.

## Avoid
**Never take a share of ongoing duty savings on live entries** (incentivises aggressive positions → 19 USC 1592 penalties, 19 CFR 111 reasonable-care problems). No paying client duty from balance sheet (importer holds own Periodic Monthly Statement). No FTZ/financing early.

## Working-capital traps
Contingency cash 9–18 months after signature; refund goes to IOR not us (need direction-to-pay); duty fronting ($2M duty ≈ $170K/month); clawback reserves 10–15%. Peak WC for 30 customers ~$300–800K (D).
