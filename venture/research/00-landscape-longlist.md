# 00 — Opportunity Landscape: Initial Long List

Date: 2026-09-30. Status: pre-research. Everything here is a hypothesis (grade D) until the
landscape analysts return evidence.

Method: start from *problems created or sharpened by structural change*, not from industries.
Each candidate is tagged with the structural change it depends on. Candidates were then assigned to
seven independent Sonnet research analysts by territory (see `01-landscape-findings.md`).

| # | Candidate | Structural change it depends on | First-pass worry |
|---|-----------|--------------------------------|------------------|
| 1 | AI-native customs brokerage for the post-de-minimis parcel economy | Section 321 de minimis suspension (2025) pushed ~1B+ parcels/yr into formal/informal entry | Carriers and cross-border platforms may already own this |
| 2 | Duty recovery (drawback, protests, PSCs, FTZ, first-sale) | US duty collections multiplied in 2025; drawback historically under-claimed | Temporary spike if tariffs roll back; incumbents exist |
| 3 | Tariff working-capital financing / bond sufficiency | Importers pay duties up front; bond insufficiencies surged | Credit risk, capital heavy |
| 4 | Interconnection / "speed-to-power" intelligence for large loads | Data-center load requests swamp utilities | Crowded (Paces, Enverus, Grid Status…) |
| 5 | Distribution-transformer remanufacturing network | Multi-year transformer lead times | Capex, ops-heavy, niche margin |
| 6 | Data-center load flexibility / VPP | Grid headroom if loads curtail | Well-funded entrants (Emerald AI) |
| 7 | Home-resilience retrofit verification + insurance-linked financing | Homeowners insurance crisis; FORTIFIED discounts | Regulated, slow carriers |
| 8 | Subrogation / claims-leakage recovery for mid-size carriers | Loss-cost inflation | Verisk, established vendors |
| 9 | Medicaid work-requirement verification for states/MCOs/providers | OBBBA (July 2025) work requirements + 6-month redeterminations | Govt procurement, political risk |
| 10 | Pharmacy/340B/MFP cash-flow tools | IRA Maximum Fair Price effectuation 2026 | Narrow, litigation-dependent |

Anti-hype pre-commitment: a candidate is eliminated if its only argument is "AI is growing", "the
TAM is huge", or "there is no competition". Each survivor must have (a) a named payer with budget,
(b) a structural "why now", and (c) a wedge a 3–5 person team can sell within 90 days.
