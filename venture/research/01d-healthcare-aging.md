# 01d — Landscape: Healthcare Admin, Public Benefits, Aging (Sonnet analyst report, 2026-09-30)

Analyst caveat: cms.gov and kffhealthnews.org blocked by proxy; evidence is from search summaries (secondary).

## Key facts
- OBBBA (P.L. 119-21) Medicaid work requirements: 80 hrs/month for expansion adults 19–64; states must implement by Jan 1, 2027; CMS interim final rule CMS-2454-IFC issued June 1, 2026; 43 states + DC affected (A-, NACo, Holland & Knight).
- Early adopters: Nebraska live May 1, 2026; Arkansas soft launch July 2026; Iowa Dec 1, 2026 (B+).
- CMS estimates ~2.3M lose coverage in 2027, ~3.2M/yr after; CBO: work requirements drive 5.3M of 10M added uninsured by 2034 (A-, KFF/CBPP).
- Rural Health Transformation: $50B/5 yrs; first-year awards avg ~$200M/state (A-, AHA/KFF).
- Vendors: Deloitte incumbent in 16 states; Maximus 17 expansion states (B+). Fortuna Health: $18M a16z round July 2025, ~$22M total (A-).
- 340B rebate pilot: enjoined Dec 2025, vacated Feb 10, 2026; relaunched via Federal Register Aug 3, 2026, effective Jan 1, 2027 (A-).
- MFP effectuation began Jan 1, 2026; NCPA-commissioned estimate ~$43K/yr lost revenue per pharmacy (B, trade-funded).
- CMS-0057-F: payer FHIR prior-auth APIs by Jan 1, 2027 (A-). Provider-side PA/RCM is crowded: Cohere ~$213M, Anterior $64M, Adonis $95M, Latent $80M Series A (B).
- Wealth transfer: Cerulli $124T by 2048 (A-). Empathy ~$162M raised (B+).
- SNAP: state admin cost-share rises 50%→75% on Oct 1, 2026; from FY2028 states with payment error ≥6% pay 5–15% of benefit costs (A-, TAMU PERC).
