# 01a — Landscape: US Trade, Customs & Tariffs (Sonnet analyst report, 2026-09-30)

Analyst caveat: cbp.gov / cbo.gov blocked for fetch; findings from law-firm/government-derived search results.

## Key facts
- **IEEPA tariffs struck down**: SCOTUS 6-3, Learning Resources v. Trump, Feb 20, 2026 (A; CRS, Skadden, Holland & Knight).
- ~$166B IEEPA duties paid by ~330,000 importers on >53M entries (A/B). CIT ordered refunds (A). CBP "CAPE" refund process in ACE: Phase 1 Apr 20, 2026; Phase II ~June 29, 2026 (A/B). Refunds not automatic; liquidated entries need a protest within 180 days of liquidation, else CIT litigation (B).
- Refund progress reportedly $134.7B accepted / ~$122B certified by Sept 11, 2026 (C, aggregator).
- **Replacement regime**: Section 122 10% surcharge Feb 24–Jul 24, 2026 (A). "Forced-labor" Section 301 tariffs of 10–12.5% on 60 economies (~99.4% of imports) effective Jul 24, 2026, no expiry/cap (A). Section 232 steel/aluminum/copper restructured Apr 6, 2026 to apply to full customs value (50% core; 25% derivatives with >15% metal content) (A); furniture/cabinets 25% rising Jan 2027; semis 25% (Jan 2026); pharma 100% (generics exempt) (A/B).
- Customs revenue FY2025 ~$195B (+150% YoY) (A); FY2026 through Aug $167.3B gross (A/B); June 2026 gross $23.6B (~$280B annualised, C).
- **De minimis**: suspended worldwide Aug 29, 2025 (A); retained post-SCOTUS on non-IEEPA authority (B); statutory repeal in OBBBA effective Jul 1, 2027 (B). 1.36B Section 321 shipments in FY2024 (B).
- **Drawback**: 99% refund, 5-yr window (A). Section 122 eligible (B); 232 historically excluded, pharma 232 reportedly allows (B). CBP processed >$1.7B/yr (B); widely repeated claim ~80–85% (~$15B) of eligible drawback unclaimed (B, original source not retrieved). Contingency 10–25% (C).
- **Brokers**: ~11–14K licensed (C); April 2026 CBLE pass rate 22% on 1,132 examinees (A). US brokerage market ~$4.2–5.5B (C). Flexport brokerage gross profit doubled in 2025 (B).
- **Startups**: Gaia Dynamics $7M seed Sept 2026 (A/B); Zonos $69M A (B); Altana ~$322M (B); Amari AI $4.5M (B); Caspian $5.4M seed (A/B); Flexport ~$2B+ raised.
- **Bonds**: record ~$3.5–3.6B bond insufficiencies FY2025 (A/B, CNBC); continuous bond amounts up 200%+ (B).
