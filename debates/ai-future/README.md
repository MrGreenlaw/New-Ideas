# Multi-agent AI debate: Should we trust where AI is headed?

An experiment in structured, multi-agent debate: two independently
briefed personas — a safety-focused skeptic and a technology-optimist
builder, each with genuinely well-founded, evidence-backed positions —
argue the future of AI, with a third persona (an economist who lost his
company to an AI-agent-enabled cyberattack) introduced mid-conversation
to pressure-test both sides from lived experience.

Read in order:

1. [`00-research-brief.md`](./00-research-brief.md) — the research
   grounding both sides in current (August 2026) evidence: expert risk
   estimates, real AI-agent security incidents, and measured productivity
   and labor-market data.
2. [`01-personas.md`](./01-personas.md) — the full system prompts given
   to each of the three debating agents.
3. [`transcript.md`](./transcript.md) — the debate itself, written turn
   by turn by separately spawned agents, each reading only the transcript
   so far before responding, plus a closing synthesis.
