# 03 — Landing page copy (redbud.example — name and domain not yet cleared)

**Headline:** Your home insurance, in plain dollars.

**Sub:** Storm-country homeowners are paying more every year and understanding less. Send us a photo of your policy. We'll
tell you what you're really paying for, what your hail deductible means in dollars, and whether there's something better.
If there isn't, we'll say so.

**How it works**
1. Snap your declarations page and renewal notice.
2. We read it and show you the important numbers — premium change, wind/hail deductible in dollars, how your roof is covered.
3. A licensed Oklahoma agent checks the carriers we work with and gives you a straight recommendation: stay or switch.
4. Every year before renewal, we check again.

**What it costs:** Nothing. If you switch, the insurance company pays us a commission. We never charge you a fee, and we
never pressure you.

**Our promise:** We'll tell you the truth even when it doesn't pay us.

**FAQ (excerpts)**
- *Are you an insurance company?* No. We're an independent agency. We work with several insurers so we can compare.
- *Will you sell my information?* No.
- *What if my current policy is the best option?* Then we'll tell you, and help you understand it.
- *Do you work with my roofer?* Some local roofers let their customers know we exist. They don't get paid for it.

**Primary CTA:** "Check my policy"  **Secondary:** "Ask a question"

Measurement: visits → uploads → completed reviews → recommendations → binds. Only *uploads* and *binds* count as evidence.
