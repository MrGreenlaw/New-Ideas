# 07 — Founding team and hiring specs

## Founder: Elliot Greenlaw — CEO / product / design / engineering, licensed producer
Owns the brand, the app, the content, and the honest-advice standard. Gaps: insurance depth, carrier relationships,
time (student), full-time availability for storm-season servicing.

## Co-founder (critical path): Licensed P&C agent — COO / Head of Agency
- **Responsibility:** producer of record, carrier relationships and appointments, quoting quality, compliance, service.
- **Expertise:** 5–15 years personal lines in Oklahoma; CISR or CIC preferred; knows which carriers write which roofs;
  ideally left a captive agency because of sales pressure (values match). A portable, verifiable book is a strong plus.
- **Time:** part-time in pilot, full-time once ~250 households or pre-seed closes.
- **When:** recruit during days 1–60. Equity-heavy (meaningful co-founder equity with vesting), modest salary later.
- **Why:** every analyst (killer, VC, regulatory) identified this as the single change that most raises the odds
  (VC estimate: P($1M revenue) ~12% → ~30–35% with this co-founder; D).
