# 02 — Outreach scripts (trust over pressure)

Voice rules: plain, warm, specific. No false urgency, no "limited time", no fear selling. Always say who we are and that
commissions come from insurers. Never promise savings.

## Neighbourhood post (research phase, before licence)
> Hi neighbours — I'm Elliot, an engineering student in Edmond. Like a lot of you, my family has watched home insurance
> climb every year. I'm researching why, and what actually helps. If your renewal jumped recently, would you be willing to
> spend 15 minutes telling me what happened? I'm not selling anything. Thank you either way.

## Follow-up to an interviewee who asked for help (after licence + carrier access)
> Thanks again for talking with me. You asked whether I could look at your policy. I'm now a licensed insurance agent
> (Oklahoma licence #____), working with [agency]. If you'd like, send a photo of your declarations page and renewal notice.
> I'll explain it in plain dollars and check what else is available. If staying where you are is the best choice, I'll
> tell you so. There's no fee; if you switch, the insurer pays us a commission.

## Realtor / loan officer email
> Subject: Insurance at closing in Oklahoma
> Hi [Name] — I'm Elliot with Redbud, a small independent insurance agency in Edmond. We focus on one thing: getting your
> buyers a clear, honest homeowners quote fast, with the wind/hail deductible shown in dollars so nobody is surprised.
> Could I ask you three questions about where insurance slows down your closings? 15 minutes, your schedule.
> (We can't pay referral fees, and we don't ask for them.)

## Roofer conversation opener
> You just gave this family a brand-new roof. Many insurers price roof age heavily, and many homeowners never tell their
> insurer about the new roof. We'd like to offer your customers a free new-roof insurance review — a one-page record of the
> roof you installed, plus an honest look at their policy. You get nothing from us for it, and you'll never be put in a
> position of talking about insurance. Would you be open to trying it with five customers?

## Independent agent partnership pitch
> I'm building intake software that turns a photo of a dec page into a clean, structured file and a plain-dollar summary.
> I'd like to run a 30-day pilot where I bring renewal-shock homeowners and you are the licensed producer of record. You keep
> your standard commission split; I learn. Interested?
