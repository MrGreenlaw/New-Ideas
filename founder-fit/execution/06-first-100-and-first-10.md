## First 100: where they come from (sources, not lists)
1. 30 interviewees (guide §A) → ask each for permission to follow up once licensed.
2. Edmond, Deer Creek, Oakdale, Piedmont, Mustang neighbourhood groups (Facebook/Nextdoor) — research posts.
3. Church community: Elliot's own congregation and OCU community — with leaders' permission, an educational "understand your
   home insurance" evening (no selling at the event; sign-up sheet for follow-up only).
4. 8 realtors / loan officers in Edmond and North OKC (Keller Williams Elite, local credit unions' mortgage teams, etc.).
5. 6 roofers with good reputations (Google 4.8+, BBB, long tenure; prefer FORTIFIED-certified roofers listed by OID).
6. Local news/explainers: a plain-language "Why did my Oklahoma home insurance go up?" article + calculator on the site.

## First 10 customers — profile and approach
- **Who:** H1 households in Edmond/North OKC who were interviewed, had a ≥15% increase, and asked for help.
- **Why them:** warm, trust already established, measurable before/after, close enough to meet at the kitchen table.
- **Approach:** after licence + appointments, send the follow-up script; meet in person; deliver the printed plain-dollar
  summary; honest stay-or-switch recommendation; if staying is right, say so (and record it — those households become the
  best referrers).
- **Success = 10 bound households from ≤40 completed reviews, with ≥3 coming from a referral.**
