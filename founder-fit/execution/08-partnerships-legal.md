# 08 — Partnerships and legal/regulatory checklist

## Partnership targets
| Partner | Why | Ask | Constraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aggregator (Smart Choice, SIAA, others) | Carrier access day 1 | Appointment; written carrier menu & commission schedule | Split 10–30%; check book-ownership terms before signing |
| Openly (direct appointment) | Agent-only carrier writing in OK (B/C) | Appointment | Appetite rules |
| Local independent agency | Producer-of-record during pilot | 30-day pilot | Their split |
| Realtors, loan officers | Closing-moment demand | Introductions | **No referral fees tied to sales** (36 O.S. §1204, §1435.14) |
| Roofers | New-roof moment | Introductions; goodwill roof record | No compensation; counsel review of any free software |
| OID consumer education | Credibility, accurate info | Use official materials (Strengthen Oklahoma Homes, consumer guides) | Never imply endorsement |

## Legal / regulatory checklist
- [ ] Oklahoma P&C producer licence (Pearson VUE exam; NIPR application) — Elliot.
- [ ] Agency (business entity) licence with Designated Responsible Licensed Producer.
- [ ] E&O policy ($1M/$1M typical).
- [ ] Counsel opinion: anti-rebating (36 O.S. §1204(8); OID Bulletin 2025-01); payments to unlicensed persons (§1435.14);
      roofer interaction (59 O.S. §1151.30 bars roofers promising to cover deductibles); free software to partners.
- [ ] Clerical vs licensed activity boundary for the app (36 O.S. §1435) — no quoting/advice without a licensed human.
- [ ] Privacy notice, data retention, security controls for dec pages; Gramm-Leach-Bliley privacy obligations for agencies.
- [ ] Advertising review: no savings promises; disclose commission compensation.
- [ ] Aggregator contract: who owns renewals/book if we leave.
- [ ] Later: non-resident licences (TX, KS), MGA licensing (OK 36 O.S. §1435.2 definitions; TX Ins. Code ch. 4053).
Items marked with statutes are cited in `research/W2_C5_regulatory_tech.md` and `research/W3_new_roof_moment.md`.
