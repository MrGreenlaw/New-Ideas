# 04 — Experiment engine (pre-registered)

Each experiment: hypothesis · test · metric · threshold · failure condition · next action.
Everything below is **UNVALIDATED** until run.

| # | Hypothesis | Test | Metric | Success threshold | Failure condition | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | Renewal shock is common and unresolved | 30 homeowner interviews (guide §A) | % with ≥15% increase in last 12 mo who did not successfully re-shop | ≥40% | <20% | Pass → E2. Fail → KILL agency thesis; fall back (see blueprint) |
| E2 | Homeowners don't understand their bill | Same interviews | % who can state wind/hail deductible in dollars | ≤40% can | ≥70% can | Pass → plain-dollar summary is a real hook. Fail → drop scanner as wedge |
| E3 | A new agency can access carriers that write OK homes | Apply to Smart Choice + SIAA; request Openly appointment; get carrier menus & commission schedules **in writing** | # carriers writing OK HO on 10–20-yr roofs via our access; new-business commission after split | ≥3 carriers; ≥10% new after split | ≤1 carrier, or <8% after split | Fail → KILL (no product to sell) |
| E4 | Re-shopping often beats the incumbent policy | 20 real comparisons by licensed producer (partner agent until Elliot licensed) | % of files with ≥15% saving or materially better coverage at ≤ same price | ≥30% | <15% | Fail → KILL; the market is priced efficiently and the agency has nothing to offer |
| E5 | People will share their dec page with us | Landing page + neighbourhood posts + interview follow-ups | Uploads per 100 serious conversations | ≥25 | <10 | Fail → test realtor channel only |
| E6 | Concierge converts | Present honest recommendations | Binds / recommended switches | ≥35% | <15% | Pass → build app; Fail → diagnose (price, trust, timing) |
| E7 | Partners will introduce us without pay | 8 realtors/LOs + 6 roofers | # who make ≥1 real introduction in 30 days | ≥3 partners | 0 | Fail → channel is content/organic only; raises CAC assumption |
| E8 | Acquisition cost is viable | Track all spend & hours per bound household | Blended CAC (incl. founder hours at $25/h) | ≤$400 | >$800 | Fail → SHELVE as venture; may remain a good small agency |
| E9 | Retention ≥85% | Cohort tracking (100-day + year 1) | 12-month household retention | ≥85% | <75% | Fail → re-examine service model |
| E10 | Roof-moment channel adds value | 5 roofers × new-roof reviews | % of new-roof homeowners whose carrier had NOT repriced; median saving | ≥40% not repriced; ≥$500 median | ≥60% repriced or <$300 | Fail → drop roofer channel (keep record as goodwill only) |

Decision rule at day 30: E1, E3, E4 must all pass to continue. Any one failing → KILL the agency thesis.
Decision rule at day 100: E6 ≥35%, E8 ≤$400, ≥50 bound households → raise pre-seed or continue bootstrapped.
