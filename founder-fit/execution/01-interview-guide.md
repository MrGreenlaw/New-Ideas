# 01 — Customer interview guide (UNVALIDATED until interviews happen)

Rule: these are research conversations, not sales calls. Until Elliot holds an Oklahoma P&C producer licence, he does
**not** quote, compare policies, or advise on coverage (36 O.S. §1435). He listens and records.

## A. Homeowners with a recent renewal increase (target: 30 in 30 days)
Recruit: Edmond / OKC neighbourhood Facebook groups, Nextdoor, church small groups (ask permission of the group leader;
never pitch from the pulpit), family friends, OCU staff, local Reddit. Offer nothing of value in exchange (anti-inducement),
just thanks.

Opening: "I'm an engineering student at [school] looking at why Oklahoma home insurance has become so expensive. I'm not
selling anything today. Could I ask you about your last renewal?"

1. When did your home insurance last renew? What happened to the price? (Get numbers: old → new premium.)
2. What did you do when you saw it? (Called the carrier / agent / shopped online / nothing.) Why?
3. If you shopped: how many quotes, how long did it take, what happened?
4. Do you know your wind/hail deductible? In dollars? (Do not correct them; note whether they know.)
5. Do you know whether your roof is covered at replacement cost or actual cash value?
6. How old is your roof? Have you replaced it? Did you tell your insurer? Did the price change?
7. Have you had a hail claim in the last 3 years? How did it go?
8. Who do you trust for insurance advice? Why? What would make you distrust someone?
9. If someone offered to read your policy, explain it in plain dollars, and re-shop it every year — for free, paid by the
   insurer's commission — what would you need to believe to say yes? What would make you say no?
10. Would you be willing to show me your declarations page? (Only collect with consent; redact personal identifiers.)

Record: premium change, deductible awareness (Y/N), ACV/RCV awareness (Y/N), roof age, claim history, shopped (Y/N),
hours spent, trusted source, stated objection, willingness to share dec page (Y/N).

## B. Independent agents in OKC/Edmond (target: 10)
1. Which carriers are actually writing new homeowners business in Oklahoma right now? On what roof ages?
2. What share of renewal-shock calls can you materially improve (≥15% saving or better coverage at same price)?
3. How long does a homeowners re-shop take you, start to finish?
4. What do you earn on a $5,500 homeowners policy, new and renewal? Aggregator or direct?
5. What do you hate about your quoting/servicing tools?
6. Would you partner (licensed producer of record) with a student building better intake software? On what terms?

## C. Realtors and loan officers (target: 8)
1. How often does insurance delay or kill a closing in Oklahoma now? Examples?
2. Who do you send buyers to for insurance today? Why them?
3. What would make you send buyers to someone new? (No referral fees can be paid — confirm that is acceptable.)

## D. Roofers (target: 6)
1. After a re-roof, do your customers ask about insurance? Do you tell them to notify their insurer?
2. Would you introduce a "new-roof insurance review" to customers with no compensation, if it made customers happier?
3. What would worry you about doing that?
