## Key verified facts (shared evidence base)

1. **Oklahoma has a FORTIFIED roof grant programme.** Strengthen Oklahoma Homes (SOH / "OKReady"), run by the Oklahoma Insurance Department (OID): up to $10,000 per primary residence toward IBHS FORTIFIED Roof; statewide in all 77 counties since Jan 2026; needs homestead exemption, active homeowners + wind policy; first-come-first-served; 500th FORTIFIED home announced 2026-09-01; participants report ~ $750/yr average premium savings; OID says "more than $4 million" invested; new application round opened 2026-07-13. (A) Sources: https://www.oid.ok.gov/release_090126/ , https://www.oid.ok.gov/release_071026/ , https://www.oid.ok.gov/release_011226/ , https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/southcentral/2026/01/13/854151.htm
   - C: $10k grant vs $750/yr saving = ~13-year payback without the grant; the programme is small (500 homes) relative to Oklahoma's housing stock. Funding source for the grant not stated in OID releases I fetched.
2. **2026 OID legislative package** (announced Dec 2025): no non-renewal/refusal solely for roof age 15+ (with inspection appeal; no denial if an authorised inspection shows 5+ years of life left), mandatory discounts for IBHS FORTIFIED homes, no denial based solely on aerial imagery, "Homeowner Bill of Rights" claim timelines (decision 30 days vs 60), quarterly non-renewal reporting, mandatory mediation. (A that it was PROPOSED: https://www.oid.ok.gov/release_121025/ , https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/southcentral/2025/12/16/851203.htm ). **Whether each item was enacted: NOT VERIFIED** (searches returned only the proposal; a House bill HB2933 was referenced as a vehicle). Treat enactment as unknown (C).
3. **Hail disputes in Oklahoma**: NPR (Apr 2026) via search summary: 600+ lawsuits pending against State Farm over hail/wind roof claims, Oklahoma AG joined a suit (B; NPR page returned 503 on direct fetch, so relies on search snippet) https://www.npr.org/2026/04/28/nx-s1-5793997/state-farm-home-insurance-hail-climate-change . ## Candidates

### 1. FORTIFIED Rails — workflow + compliance software for FORTIFIED roofers/evaluators (and grant navigation)
- **Problem / who pays:** Roofing contractors and FORTIFIED evaluators must document each roof to IBHS standards (photos, fastening/underlayment/deck steps) and, in Oklahoma, shepherd homeowners through the SOH grant (homestead exemption, policy proof, wind policy). Payers: roofing companies ($/month SaaS or per-project fee), later insurers/states funding programmes.
- **Evidence:** SOH exists, 77 counties, $10k grants, 500 homes done (A). OID proposal for mandatory FORTIFIED discounts (A as proposal; enactment unverified). FORTIFIED programmes exist beyond OK (Alabama is the origin; other states — NOT verified by me, B from background knowledge).
- **Existing solutions / gaps:** IBHS runs its own evaluator portal; generic roofing CRMs (JobNimbus, AccuLynx, Roofr) exist (B, background knowledge). Gap (C): a polished, homeowner-facing grant-and-timeline experience and contractor-side documentation checklist that is trust-first. Could easily be a feature IBHS or the CRMs add.
- **Why now:** Programme newly statewide (Jan 2026), insurer-discount mandate proposed, roofing licensing (HB 1628) forcing contractors to professionalise 2027-28.
- **30-day part-time wedge:** Free iOS/web "FORTIFIED readiness" checklist + grant eligibility screener for homeowners in Edmond/OKC; interview 10 FORTIFIED-approved roofers (OID lists participants) and offer a done-for-you photo-documentation PDF workflow for $0 pilot. No licence needed (you are not inspecting or adjusting).
- **Path to $100M+:** Honest: hard. Needs multi-state programmes (AL, LA, MS, SC, TX, etc.) plus insurer/state B2B2G contracts, or expansion into general roof-condition documentation (candidate 2). Roofing-contractor SaaS at ~$300/mo x 25,000 paying firms = $90M — requires dominating a CRM-crowded market (C/D).
- **Moat:** Programme-specific data + relationships with OID/contractors/evaluators; design craft. Weak technically.
- **Founder-fit: 7/10.** Mobile + design strength, local access to OID/contractors, no licence needed, ethical positive-sum story (helps homeowners). Penalised for tiny market size today and B2B sales burden.
- **Verdict: PURSUE as a small test (30-day), not yet as a venture.**

### 2. Roof Passport — LiDAR/photo roof & property condition record for homeowners
- **Problem / who pays:** Homeowners face non-renewal on roof age, claim denial from aerial imagery, and hail claim disputes. Dated, geotagged, high-quality evidence of roof condition (and pre-storm baseline) helps contest. Payers: homeowners (low $), but better via roofers (as a lead/trust tool), mortgage/real-estate agents, or insurers/inspectors.
- **Evidence:** OID proposal bans denial based solely on aerial imagery and allows independent inspection appeal (A as proposal). 600+ lawsuits vs State Farm (B). Roof replacement ~$17.6k avg (B).
- **Existing solutions / gaps:** Roofer inspection apps (Hover, EagleView, CompanyCam — B background), insurer drone/aerial vendors (Verisk, EagleView). Gap (C): a homeowner-owned baseline record, but homeowners rarely pay for evidence they hope never to use.
- **Why now:** Aerial-imagery underwriting controversy, non-renewals, litigation.
- **30-day wedge:** SwiftUI app: guided roof/attic/gutter photo capture, time-stamped PDF "condition report", free; distribute through 3-5 local roofers and an independent insurance agent. Must NOT give claim advice or assess value (avoids adjuster/public adjuster licensure — C; verify OK public-adjuster statute before any claim-related feature).
- **Path to $100M+:** Needs insurer/inspection channel or per-report B2B fees across hail belt (TX, KS, NE, CO, MN). Plausible but monetisation unproven (D).
- **Moat:** Weak; data accumulation across many roofs could matter if insurers accept it (D).
- **Founder-fit: 6/10.** Perfect skill match (iOS, LiDAR), but payer problem and insurer acceptance.
- **Verdict: SHELVE unless paired with candidate 1 channel.**

## Top 3 and strongest failure reason

1. **FORTIFIED Rails (candidate 1), paired with candidate 10 as cash engine** — Best fit: real programme, real contractor payers, no licence, local access.
   - *Strongest failure reason:* the market is tiny and may be captured by IBHS's own portal or by roofing CRMs; 500 homes and ~$4M invested shows the programme is small, and $750/yr savings on a $10k upgrade means demand outside the grant is weak (A/C). Could be a feature, not a company.
2. **Roof Passport (candidate 2), only if tied to the roofer/insurer channel** — Strongest skill match (iOS + LiDAR).
   - *Failure reason:* nobody pays for evidence before a loss, and insurers have no obligation to accept a startup's data; the payer problem is unresolved and the legislation protecting homeowners may not have passed (unverified).
3. **PCTC school-side toolkit (candidate 3)** — Faith-aligned, clear payer (schools), fresh programme.
   - *Failure reason:* incumbents (FACTS etc.) and the policy dependence: the credit is a political programme with an annual cap; reversal or litigation would erase the wedge, and small schools have tiny software budgets.

No candidate clears a credible $100M+ bar from Oklahoma alone; each requires multi-state replication that I have NOT validated. The most defensible use of the Oklahoma access advantage is cheap customer discovery in insurance-driven roofing (1, 2) funded by a design-craft service (10). Items needing verification before any commitment: enactment status of the 2026 OID bills; whether IBHS's portal/other vendors already serve FORTIFIED documentation; FORTIFIED programme status in other states; Oklahoma public-adjuster law boundaries.
