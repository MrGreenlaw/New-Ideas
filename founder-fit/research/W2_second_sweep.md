# W2 Second Sweep: Big-Comp Ideas for Elliot (as of 2026-10-01)

Brief: every candidate needs (1) a real $1B+ valuation or $100M+ revenue comp, (2) a verifiable 2024-2026 "why now", (3) a wedge startable in 30 days and paid within 90, with no capital and no licence beyond a weeks-long exam.

**Claim labels**
- **A** = primary source (company filing/press release, regulator, government).
- **B** = reputable secondary reporting (trade press, major outlet).
- **C** = aggregator, vendor blog, or estimate. Directional only.
- **D** = my inference or arithmetic. Must be validated.

Where a number is not known, it says "unknown" instead of a guess. Several economics (commission rates, placement fees, prices) are unknown and are the first things to validate in the 30-day wedge.

## 1. Trust-first independent insurance agency (Oklahoma home/auto), Goosehead-style

**Model.** An independent P&C agency that shops many carriers for the customer. Revenue is carrier commission, new plus renewal. Later it scales through producers, acquisitions of small agencies, or a franchise/sub-agent network.

**Comparables**
- **Goosehead Insurance:** FY2025 total revenue $365.3M (+16%), 1.9M policies in force, $4.4B written premium placed, net income $44.5M. **[A]** [FY2025 results](https://ir.gooseheadinsurance.com/news-releases/news-release-details/goosehead-insurance-inc-announces-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-5)
- Goosehead scale: about 1,068 franchises, 2,647 producing agents, 417 corporate agents, about $28K average monthly payment per franchise. **[C]** (aggregator pages) [Goosehead investor presentation, Nov 2025](https://ir.gooseheadinsurance.com/static-files/905b5006-d5c8-4280-aecc-f578efbfd734)
- **Valuation caution:** Goosehead market cap was shown as about $1.42B after the stock fell about 44% in a year. **[C]** [TIKR](https://www.tikr.com/blog/goosehead-gshd-stock-valuation-2026). The $100M+ revenue test is passed on primary data. The $1B+ valuation test is passed only marginally and is not currently a high-multiple story.
- **Baldwin Group** (independent distribution): 2025 revenue about $1.5B (+8%), adjusted EBITDA margin 23%, but a $54.2M net loss. **[A]** [Baldwin results](https://ir.baldwin.com/news-releases/news-release-details/baldwin-group-announces-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025/)
- Consolidator buyers (BroadStreet, Hub, Acrisure, Brown & Brown) drove 80%+ of broker M&A in 2024-H1 2025. Mid-market agencies with $1M+ EBITDA traded around 11-12x; small personal-lines agencies traded around 1.5-2.5x revenue. **[C]** [Renegade](https://renegadeinsurance.com/insurance-agency-valuation/), [Ad Astra](https://www.adastraequity.com/ebitda-multiples/insurance-agency)

**Why now (verified)**
- Oklahoma homeowners premiums are among the highest in the US: average about $5,298/yr, about 121% above the national average. **[C]** [LendingTree](https://www.lendingtree.com/insurance/state-of-home-insurance/)
- Oklahoma rate-change data conflict: +5.5% in 2025 (**[C]** LendingTree) versus a headline of a 14% drop (**[C]** [Live Insurance News](https://www.liveinsurancenews.com/oklahoma-most-expensive-state/8574785/)). Do not cite a single rate trend.
- Nonrenewal rate in Oklahoma reportedly up 103%. **[C]** [Valuepenguin / NerdWallet roundup](https://www.valuepenguin.com/best-cheap-homeowners-insurance-oklahoma). Verify against OID data before using it in a pitch.
- State Farm paid over $1B for Oklahoma wind/hail over two years and is facing hail-claim litigation. **[B]** [NPR, Apr 2026](https://www.npr.org/2026/04/28/nx-s1-5793997/state-farm-home-insurance-hail-climate-change), [State Farm newsroom](https://newsroom.statefarm.com/state-farm-reports-increase-in-hail-claim-costs/) **[A]**
- New Oklahoma law requires advance filing/review of homeowner rate increases; public filing required from July 1, 2027. **[B]** [Homes.com](https://www.homes.com/news/new-oklahoma-law-requires-advance-state-review-of-homeowners-insurance-rate-hikes/708014846/)
- Net reading **[D]**: confusion, nonrenewals and carrier churn make a customer-side shopper valuable. Buyers of agencies now price AI-enabled workflow (renewal comparison, cross-sell signals), which suits an engineer. **[C]** [Sonant](https://www.sonant.ai/blog/insurance-agency-valuation)

**30-day wedge**
- Oklahoma P&C producer licence: exam only, 150 questions in 2.5 hours, 70% to pass, age 18+, background check. Pre-licensing education is recommended but not required by OID. **[B]** [Xcel](https://www.xcelsolutions.com/oklahoma/insurance-license/requirements)
- Weeks 1-2: study and sit the exam. Weeks 3-4: choose a carrier-access route. Options, to be validated: (a) producer role at an existing independent agency or Goosehead corporate/franchise (Goosehead franchise cost reported at about $40K-$118K **[C]**, so the franchise route breaks the no-capital rule); (b) appointment through an aggregator or MGA.
- Product wedge: "Renewal re-shop." The customer sends the declarations page; Elliot returns a plain-language side-by-side with no pressure. It extends his OTD price-transparency instinct. Build a clean iOS/web intake tool for the declarations page (his core strength).
- Targets: his network, OC/campus staff and families, Greenturf customers, church-adjacent families (no church-management product is involved).

**Who pays / how much.** The carrier pays commission on written premium. Commission rates by carrier and line are **unknown** and must be gotten from the appointment contract. **[D]** Arithmetic only: Goosehead's revenue/premium ratio is $365.3M / $4.4B = about 8.3%, so $100M revenue corresponds to about $1.2B of placed premium at that ratio. It is not a promise of Elliot's take rate.

**Route to $100M revenue.** (1) Own book plus 2-3 producers. (2) Tech-assisted producer network or sub-agent model (Goosehead playbook). (3) Acquire retiring small agencies (valuation data above). This is a multi-year, 10+ year route. A $100M outcome requires a franchise or acquisition engine, not Elliot alone. **[D]**

**Moat.** Carrier access, retention-driven recurring book, brand trust, workflow software. Weak at first; the book is the moat. Book ownership terms in any producer contract are critical.

**Key risks**
- Commissions on personal lines are modest per policy, so early income is small.
- E&O liability, carrier appointment minimums, and non-solicit/book-ownership terms in employment deals.
- Hard-market capacity could limit what an agent can place.
- Goosehead stock weakness shows the public market is not paying a premium multiple today.
- Student time.

**Founder-fit: 7/10.** Licence by exam, trust voice, price-transparency product experience, local Oklahoma weather pain. Minus: age and credibility, sales grind.

## 2. Trades talent pipeline: contingent placement of CareerTech/trade-school grads

**Model.** A recruiter-plus-software platform that sources, screens and places apprentices and entry-level techs into HVAC, electrical, plumbing and service shops. Revenue is a placement fee and later subscription/managed apprenticeship.

**Comparables**
- **Workrise** (skilled-trade workforce platform): $300M Series E at a $2.9B valuation; gross revenue about $900M at close of 2020. **[B]** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/05/20/workrise-once-known-as-rigup-raises-300m-at-a-2-9b-valuation/), [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/research/workrise-series-e-funding/). Warning: it is a pre-2024 peak figure; the later trajectory is not verified here.
- **Handshake** (Gen Z talent network): $3.5B valuation in Jan 2022 Series F. **[B]** [Built In SF](https://www.builtinsf.com/articles/handshake-raises-200m-3b-valuation-hiring)
- **Instawork** (on-demand staffing): about $97.3M ARR in 2024, up from $55.2M. **[C]** [Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/instawork). Estimate only.

**Why now (verified)**
- Workforce Pell begins July 1, 2026: Pell eligibility extended to accredited short-term programs of 150-599 hours (8-15 weeks) in fields including skilled trades. **[B]** [SHRM](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/news/organizational-employee-development/workforce-pell-grants-become-a-reality), [UPCEA](https://upcea.edu/workforce-pell-is-now-law-under-the-one-big-beautiful-bill-obbb/)
- Vocational-focused community college enrollment rose 16% from 2022 to 2023; construction trades up 23%. **[B]** [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2024/04/22/1245858737/gen-z-trade-vocational-schools-jobs-college), citing National Student Clearinghouse
- Oklahoma CareerTech reports a 40% demand increase since COVID, receives about 3% of the state education budget, and is expanding apprenticeships; roughly 850 electrician openings/yr and 430 additional HVAC workers needed. **[A/B]** [CareerTech release](https://oklahoma.gov/careertech/media-center/press-releases/2025/careertech-calls-for-greater-investment-to-meet-growing-demand-f.html), [apprenticeship expansion](https://oklahoma.gov/careertech/media-center/press-releases/2025/oklahoma-careertech-to-play-a-leading-role-in-plans-to-expand-ap.html)
- Net reading **[D]**: public training capacity is stretched, and Workforce Pell channels funds to short programs, so there is a supply of trained entrants and shops struggling to hire.

**30-day wedge.** Pick one trade (HVAC) and one metro (OKC/Edmond). Interview 15 shop owners. Build a simple, beautifully designed candidate profile and shop-side matching page. Offer 3 shops a pay-on-hire pilot using grads from nearby technology centers. First revenue: one placement fee. Fee levels are **unknown**; validate in the interviews. **[D]** Check whether Oklahoma regulates private employment/placement agencies before charging candidates or fees. I did not verify this; it is a gating item.

**Who pays.** Shops (employer-paid). Never charge candidates (dignity-for-all values and legal safety).

**Route to $100M.** Multi-trade, multi-state placement plus managed apprenticeship (employer funds wages and training admin), plus payroll/embedded finance. Instawork's roughly $97M ARR after years of operation shows the scale needed. **[C]** Slow, and the history of Workrise shows how hard unit economics and cyclicality are. **[D]**

**Moat.** Relationships with technology centers and shop owners; placement data on who sticks. Moderate.

**Key risks.** Low take per placement, long hiring cycles, local relationships required, disintermediation (shop hires direct next time), staffing-law compliance.

**Founder-fit: 7/10.** Direct trades exposure (Greenturf), design-led candidate experience, values-aligned. Minus: B2B sales cycles during school.

## 3. Premium exterior/holiday lighting brand (franchise-able)

**Model.** Design-led install of holiday and permanent track lighting. Premium brand, sales-led, installs by trained crews or subcontractors. Later a franchise/licensing system.

**Comparables**
- **Christmas Decor** franchisor: about $75M system sales, about 200 franchisees and 240 territories at its Nov 2024 acquisition. **[A]** [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20241114183580/en/Tuckers-Farm-Corporations-Subsidiary-Wonder-Franchises-Announces-Acquisition-of-Christmas-Decor-Countrys-Leading-Christmas-Decorating-Franchisor-with-%2475-Million-in-System-Sales)
- **Neighborly** (multi-brand home-services franchisor): over $4B systemwide sales in 2023 across 19 verticals, 5,500 franchises. **[A]** [Neighborly release](https://www.neighborlybrands.com/press-center/news/2024/neighborly-surpasses-4-billion-in-systemwide-sales/)
- **Gap:** the pure-lighting comp is **under $100M**, and it took about 28 years. The $100M+ evidence is a multi-brand platform, not a lighting brand. This fails the strict test on its own.

**Why now.** Weak. Permanent lighting is projected to reach $4.7B by 2028 at about 15% CAGR, but that is a vendor/market-report figure. **[C]** [Strandr](https://www.strandr.com/blog/permanent-lighting-year-round-revenue-contractor-guide). I found no verified structural 2024-2026 shift beyond app-controlled LEDs.

**30-day wedge.** Today is Oct 1: holiday-lighting booking season. Sell 5-10 premium installs in Edmond/Nichols Hills by late October, subcontract install, deliver by early December. Paid within 90 days is realistic. Typical new-franchise first-year revenue claims of $150K-$400K are vendor-sourced. **[C]** [PulseRevOps](https://pulserevops.com/knowledge/q15477). Do not rely on them.

**Route to $100M.** Franchise system. At royalty-style revenue, $100M of company revenue would need system sales many times Christmas Decor's, so the honest route is a multi-service premium exterior brand, not lighting alone. **[D]**

**Moat.** Brand and design only. Low.

**Key risks.** Seasonality, ladder/electrical liability and insurance, crew management, low barrier to copy.

**Founder-fit: 7/10.** Greenturf experience and design taste. Best treated as a funding wedge for something larger.

## 4. Tech-enabled bookkeeping for local small businesses

**Comparables**
- **Pilot:** $1.2B valuation (2021-22); about $43M ARR in 2024. **[C]** [Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/pilot), [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/fintech/pilot-lands-unicorn-valuation-from-boosted-series-c-round). Valuation test met; revenue test not met.
- **Bench:** more than 12,000 SMB customers; shut down Dec 27, 2024; acquired by Employer.com three days later; bankruptcy filing disclosed debts over $65M. **[B]** [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2024/vancouver-fintech-company-bench-accounting-announces-sudden-shutdown/), [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/smbs/2024/online-bookkeeping-platform-bench-accounting-announces-abrupt-shutdown/)

**Why now.** The Bench collapse displaced thousands of customers and shows the category is both needed and unit-economics-fragile. **[B]** AI-native bookkeeping tools are making delivery cheaper; I did not verify specific products here. **[D]**

**30-day wedge.** No licence needed for bookkeeping (verify scope: tax advice/prep is separate). Sign 3-5 local businesses (trades, studios, church-adjacent) at a flat monthly fee. Price is **unknown**; test it.

**Route to $100M.** Only via software margins at scale; Pilot did not reach $100M ARR in about a decade. **[C]** This is the weakest path among the bookkeeping-type ideas.

**Moat.** Low. **Risk.** Margin squeeze, error liability, a crowded field. **Founder-fit: 5/10.**

## 5. Design-led printed-products brand (3D printing as the factory)

**Comparables**
- **Bambu Lab:** revenue reportedly about RMB 6B in 2024 and over RMB 10B in 2025; valuation reportedly RMB 40B. **[C]** [KrAsia](https://www.kr-asia.com/pulses/161999), [36Kr](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3531873407458439). Hardware maker, not a service model.
- **Xometry** (on-demand manufacturing marketplace): FY2025 revenue $686.6M (+25.9%), marketplace $629.6M, adjusted EBITDA $18.5M. **[A/B]** [Xometry release](https://investors.xometry.com/news-releases/news-release-details/xometry-reports-record-fourth-quarter-and-strong-full-year-2025), [3DPrint.com](https://3dprint.com/324209/3d-printing-financials-xometry-reports-record-2025-results-and-ceo-transition/)
- **Formlabs:** $2B valuation (May 2021). **[B]** [VoxelMatters](https://www.voxelmatters.com/formlabs-doubles-2-billion-valuation-with-close-of-150-million-series-e/)
- **Print-on-demand:** Printful/Printify merged (Nov 2024) as Fyul. **[C]** [beyond-print](https://www.beyond-print.net/printful-printify-fyul-a-merger-that-is-shaking-up-the-print-on-demand-market/). I am not citing the $2.3B figure sometimes quoted for Printful. It is an aggregator number and I could not verify it.

**Why now.** The US ended the de minimis duty-free threshold on Aug 29, 2025, which raises costs for imported goods; US-based production is unaffected. **[B]** [Printify help](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/33398560291729-Import-Tariffs-Updates-for-Printify-Merchants). Consumer printer unit sales roughly tripled 2024-2025 on app-download data (**[C]** [Fabbaloo](https://www.fabbaloo.com/news/app-download-data-suggests-sharp-increase-in-bambu-lab-3d-printer-sales-in-2025)), meaning cheap local production capacity.

**30-day wedge.** A small range of well-designed functional products (organisers, climbing/gym accessories, branded corporate items) printed locally, sold in person and online. Revenue in 90 days is plausible but small. A pure print-for-hire service is a commodity and Xometry shows the $100M comp is a national marketplace with a large supplier network, which is not a student startup. **[D]**

**Route to $100M.** Only via a product brand that outgrows printing (injection moulding, retail). That is a different, capital-heavy business. **Moat:** design and brand. **Risk:** low margins, copyable designs, IP. **Founder-fit: 5/10.**

## 6. Design-forward real estate brokerage

**Comparables**
- **Real Brokerage:** 2025 revenue about $2B (+56%), 31,700+ agents, 185,314 transactions. **[B]** [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/real-2025-revenue-2b/)
- **eXp World Holdings:** 2025 revenue $4.8B (+4%), 83,060 agents. **[B]** [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/real-2025-revenue-2b/), [Direct Selling News](https://www.directsellingnews.com/2026/02/25/exp-world-holdings-reports-q4-and-full-year-2025-financial-results/)

**Why now.** The NAR settlement took effect mid-July 2024: no offers of compensation on the MLS, and written buyer-agent agreements are required. **[B]** [MortgagePoint](https://themortgagepoint.com/2025/05/16/measuring-the-impact-of-nar-settlements-on-agent-commissions/). But buyer-agent commissions rebounded to about 2.42% in Q3 2025, so a "disrupt the commission" thesis is **not supported**. **[B]** [Inman](https://www.inman.com/2025/12/08/buyers-agent-commissions-see-rebound-in-wake-of-settlement/)

**30-day wedge.** Oklahoma provisional sales associate: 90 hours pre-licence education, a 120-question exam, $75 fee, background check, sponsoring broker. **[B]** [Colibri](https://www.colibrirealestate.com/real-estate/oklahoma/license-requirements/), [USRealtyTraining](https://www.usrealtytraining.com/blogs/oklahoma-real-estate-exam). Licence plus first closing within 90 days is tight **[D]**.

**Route to $100M.** The Real/eXp model needs thousands of agents and a revenue-share engine; both are agent-recruiting businesses, not software or design businesses. **Moat:** low. **Risk:** commission-only income, slow closings, saturated market. **Founder-fit: 5/10.**

## 7. Trust-first Medicare brokerage

**Comparable.** **SelectQuote:** fiscal 2026 revenue guidance cut to $1.35-1.45B (from $1.61-1.71B). **[A]** [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001794783/000179478326000027/selectquoteincmarch312026pr.htm). Revenue test met by a large margin, but the guidance cut shows a volatile market.

**Why now (mixed).** A court invalidated CMS's rule capping agent/broker compensation, and Medicare Advantage carriers are changing benefits and volumes. **[B]** [Morgan Lewis](https://www.morganlewis.com/blogs/healthlawscan/2026/02/medicare-advantage-agent-and-broker-agreements-2025-in-review). Today's date is Oct 1; the annual enrollment period normally starts Oct 15 (**[D]**, confirm), so a licence plus carrier certification plus contracting is unlikely to be done in time. First commissions would arrive after January effective dates, at best near day 90.

**Verdict.** KILL for now. The model is built on high-volume phone sales, which clashes with "no manipulative urgency", and the target customers are seniors. **Founder-fit: 3/10.**

- Test for #1 and #2 in parallel in October, and decide by day 30 on the basis of three numbers: licence/appointment secured, first 10 customer conversations, and actual per-policy or per-placement revenue.
