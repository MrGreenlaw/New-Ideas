# Final thesis under test (brief for final-round analysts)

Working name: Redbud (name not yet cleared).

One line: an honest homeowners-and-auto insurance agency for storm country that reads your policy in plain dollars,
re-shops it at every renewal across every carrier it can access, tells you when staying put is the right answer, and
gets better at placing each home with every home it places.

Wedge ("Renewal Check"): an Oklahoma homeowner facing a renewal increase photographs the declarations page / renewal
notice in an iPhone app or on the web. Software extracts premium, coverages, wind/hail deductible (shown in dollars, not
%), roof settlement (ACV vs RCV), and flags gaps. A licensed agent (founder is licensed; co-founder is an experienced P&C
agent) re-shops through an aggregator (Smart Choice/SIAA) plus direct appointments such as Openly, and gives an honest
"stay or switch" recommendation. Free to the homeowner. Revenue = carrier commissions (~12–15% new, 8–12% renewal, minus
aggregator split ~10–30%). Oklahoma average homeowners premium ≈ $5.4–5.9k (A/B). Bundle auto to raise revenue/household
and retention.

Channels: renewal-season local content; realtors and mortgage loan officers (binder needed at closing); roofers ("new-roof
insurance review", no compensation); community/word of mouth. Anti-rebating (36 O.S. §1204) and no commissions to
unlicensed persons (§1435.14) constrain all partner incentives.

Long term: placement intelligence (which carrier takes which home at what price) → lower cost per bind and higher hit
rate; multi-state hail belt (OK, TX, KS, NE, CO, MO, AR, MN: $36.6B homeowners premium, NAIC 2024 — A); possible MGA for
verified new-roof homes ONLY if loss data supports it (no hail-specific FORTIFIED loss evidence exists today).
Comparables: Goosehead (2025 revenue ~$365M, ~86% retention, market cap ~$1.6–2.4B in 2026), Porch Group (software →
insurance distribution → reciprocal; targeting $600M reciprocal premium 2026), Baldwin Group, Matic, Neptune (MGA, IPO ~$2.8B).

Known weaknesses already found: weak early moat; carrier appointment/appetite risk for older hail-exposed roofs; thin per-
policy economics (~$420–650/yr gross to agency); paid-lead CAC ~$120–250 per bind (vendor) to $600+ (realistic); digital
agencies' mixed history (Policygenius, Gabi, Young Alfred); Oklahoma law blunts post-claim switching (36 O.S. §3639.1,
OAC 365:15-7-26); founder has no insurance background; student time.
