### Verified in my own checks
- 36 O.S. §961–962: premium discount for FORTIFIED-certified retrofits (A — Justia statute text). Discount size is carrier-filed (up to ~42% of wind/hail portion per OID one-sheet; B).
- TDI Commissioner's Bulletin B-0008-26 (16 Sept 2026) *encourages* actuarially supported mitigation discounts and asks insurers to account for FORTIFIED status in rating; it does not mandate a discount (A — tdi.texas.gov).
- Neptune Insurance (flood MGA): IPO Oct 2025 at $20/share, ~$2.8B target valuation; 2025 revenue $159.6M, net income $37.4M (A — SEC filings, Insurance Journal).
- Porch Group: inspector/home-services software → homeowners insurance; formed Porch Insurance Reciprocal Exchange Jan 2025; Q2 2025 revenue $107M; targeting $600M reciprocal written premium in 2026 (A — SEC/Business Wire). This is the closest structural comparable to "vertical workflow software as insurance distribution".

## Debate protocol
**Disagreement:** the blind second sweep ranks the agency #1; the market analyst sizes the Oklahoma agency at ~$0.4M/yr by year 3; the killer kills the MGA.
**Root cause:** they are describing different things. The killer killed the *differentiation claim* (FORTIFIED/resilience as edge) and the *Neptune analogy*. Nobody killed the *payer* (carrier commissions on a very large, inflating premium pool: $36.6B homeowners premium across eight hail-belt states, OK $2.56B — A, NAIC 2024). The market analyst's small SOM assumes generic acquisition (paid leads at $120–600 per bind) with a single producer.
**What would resolve it:** a *structurally cheaper acquisition channel* that lands on the *most placeable* risks. If one exists, the agency's unit economics and ceiling change; if not, it's a nice small business.

## The reframe that came out of the debate: the new-roof moment
Combining what died in round 1 (roof record — nobody pays) with what survived round 2 (carrier commissions are a real payer):
- A brand-new roof is the moment a hail-belt home's insurability is highest: roof age resets (roof age is a primary underwriting/rating variable — B), carriers that decline old roofs want new ones (C), the homeowner has often just had a hail claim with their current carrier (C), and nobody prompts a re-shop (D).
- The roofer is present at exactly that moment. A free, excellent job-completion app ("roof record") gives the roofer a reason to involve the agency; the agency's commission pays for everything.
- Comparable: Porch (inspectors at the moment of home purchase).

Round 3 tests this with an evidence analyst and a dedicated killer. C7 (trades talent pipeline) is being tested in parallel as the alternative.
