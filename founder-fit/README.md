## Verdict in one paragraph
About 60 candidates were generated, and 30 independent Sonnet analysts examined them over four rounds. The opportunity that best combines evidence, a real payer and founder fit is **Redbud**: an honest, licensed, independent home-and-auto insurance agency for storm country, starting in Edmond/OKC. Its wedge is the annual **renewal shock**. Redbud reads the policy in plain dollars, re-shops it, and recommends "stay" when staying is right. Carrier commissions pay for it (≈ $400–650 per household per year). It is **a strong small business with a venture option, not a proven $10B company**. The base case is ≈ $2.2M revenue in year 5; a funded three-state case reaches ≈ $16M. A $1B+ outcome would require a later underwriting layer that today's evidence does not yet justify. **Verdict: PURSUE THE TEST, NOT YET THE COMPANY.** A 30-day, ~$300, ~40-hour test with pre-registered kill criteria settles the crux. **Nothing is customer-validated yet.**
