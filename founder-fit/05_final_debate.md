# 05 — Final-round debate resolution

Inputs: `research/W3_new_roof_moment.md`, `W3_killer.md`, `W4_VC_final.md`, `W4_business_model.md`, my model (`model/`).

## D1. Is the roofer "new-roof moment" the wedge?
- Evidence analyst: **partly supported**. Risk drops (Verisk: poor/moderate roofs ≈ 60% higher loss costs; B). But post-claim switching is blunted by Oklahoma law (36 O.S. §3639.1 bars non-renewal or surcharge solely for a first claim; OAC 365:15-7-26 bars weather-claim surcharges; A). Insurance-funded roofs (~21% of jobs; C) are already known to the carrier.
- Killer: **KILL the wedge**. Roofers have no lawful financial incentive (§1204, §1435.14, 59 O.S. §1151.30). Carriers may already re-rate from permits and imagery.
- **Resolution:** both agree on the facts and differ only on salvage. The roofer channel is demoted to a small, counsel-approved probe (E10). The **renewal notice** becomes the primary trigger, because it reaches *every* household every year, with no partner incentive needed.

## D2. My model vs the business-model analyst
- My first base case: ~$11M revenue in year 5. Analyst: ~$2.06M.
- **Cause:** I assumed new-household volume of 150 → 9,000/yr and $90/policy servicing; the analyst assumed ~120 → ~1,000/yr and costed licensed CSRs (~60% gross margin).
- **Resolution:** the analyst's volumes are better grounded for a part-time founder in one state. I re-based the model (base now $2.19M in year 5) and moved my original volumes into a funded, three-state aggressive case. That is the honest correction.

## D3. VC: "build the trades studio now; run Redbud as a side test" vs blueprint: "run the Redbud test first"
- **Cause of disagreement:** the VC optimises for near-term cash and a founder who needs income. The blueprint optimises for *irreversible learning per hour*.
- **Assessment:** the Redbud test is mostly licensure plus conversations, costs ~$300, and settles the crux in 30 days. The studio has no crux to settle; we already know it works as a small cash business. Running the studio first spends Elliot's scarce hours on something whose answer is already known.
- **Resolution:** run the Redbud 30-day test first. **The studio is the pre-registered fallback** if the day-30 kill criteria fire, or a parallel income source only if Elliot needs cash and has hours beyond the test. Uncertainty preserved: if Elliot's real constraint is income rather than learning, the VC's ordering is correct.

## D4. Is there any $10B path?
- Every analyst said no for an agency. The only $1B+ route is an underwriting layer (Neptune ~$2.8B IPO on ~$160M revenue; Porch's reciprocal), and today's evidence does not support one for hail (no hail-specific FORTIFIED loss data; Hippo's hail losses).
- **Resolution:** the blueprint states plainly that $10B is not conceivable for this model. Multi-billion requires future evidence this company could uniquely generate: its own placement and loss data.
