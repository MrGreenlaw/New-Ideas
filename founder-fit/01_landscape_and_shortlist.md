Date: 2026-10-01. Inputs: five independent Sonnet landscape analysts, each with a different lens
(`research/L1_trades.md`, `L2_spatial.md`, `L3_trust_faith.md`, `L4_oklahoma_shifts.md`, `L5_contrarian.md`),
plus my own verification searches. Roughly 55 candidates were generated in total.

Evidence grades: A = directly verified (source), B = strongly supported, C = reasonable inference, D = speculative.

## 1. What the landscape said

### Convergence (and why I distrust it a little)
Four of five analysts (L1, L2, L4, L5) independently put an **Oklahoma roof / hail documentation** idea in their top two.
That is a signal, but a contaminated one: my briefs for L1, L2 and L4 mentioned Oklahoma hail, and the shared founder
profile mentions Oklahoma. Only L5 had no hail prompt. So: convergence = "worth a deep dive", not "validated".

### A load-bearing claim that turned out to be false
Three analysts leaned on the Oklahoma Insurance Department's 2026 legislative package ("no non-renewal solely for
roof age 15+", "no denial based solely on aerial imagery", "independent inspection appeal", "mandatory FORTIFIED
discounts") as the *why now* for roof documentation. I checked. The vehicle bill, **HB 2933, passed the House but died
in the Senate on 14 May 2026** (A — BillTrack50 history; oklegislature.gov bill page; nondoc.com 2026-05-14 on the
Senate blocking House bills). What *did* pass was **HB 3781 (prior-approval rate review, effective 1 July 2027)** (A —
Insurance Journal 2026-05-14). So the roof-documentation thesis lost its strongest legal hook. The pain (hail, roof-age
underwriting, claim disputes) remains real; the regulatory forcing function does not exist yet in Oklahoma.
This is exactly the kind of "consensus built on one unverified assumption" the brief warns about.

### Other verified facts that matter
- Oklahoma: 49.7% of roofs hit by severe hail over four years; average roof replacement $17,631 in 2025 (A — OID release of Verisk data, oid.ok.gov/release_062926).
- Strengthen Oklahoma Homes (FORTIFIED roof grants up to $10,000; statewide since Jan 2026; 500th home Sept 2026) (A — OID).
- Oklahoma AG suits against State Farm and Allstate over hail/wind claims, 2026 (A per L1/L5 citations; B for the "600+ suits" count).
- Oklahoma residential roofing endorsement (HB 1628): transition 2027-01-01, full enforcement 2028-01-01 (A — oklahoma.gov/cib).
- US severe-convective-storm insured losses >$50B for the third straight year (2025: ~$51B) (A — Triple-I).
- FTC CARS Rule vacated (5th Cir., Jan 2025) and withdrawn (Feb 2026) (A per L3).
- Oklahoma Parental Choice Tax Credit: $5,000–$7,500/student, cap ~$252–275M, ~40k students; accredited private schools 119 → 192 (2021-22 → 2025-26) (A/B per L3, L4).
- **Federal tax-credit scholarship (ECCA, in P.L. 119-21, signed 4 July 2025): $1,700 individual dollar-for-dollar credit for gifts to Scholarship Granting Organizations, no national cap, states opt in, starts 1 Jan 2027; IRS Rev. Proc. 2026-6 lets states make an advance election** (A — IRS newsroom, CRS R48724, EdChoice). This is the single largest structural change any analyst surfaced, and it lands in 3 months.
- Xactimate shipped LiDAR sketch scanning (June 2026) (B per L5) → standalone "LiDAR to estimate" apps are commoditised.
- AI voice receptionists for trades are a funded land-grab (Avoca reported ~$1B valuation) (B) → rejected.
